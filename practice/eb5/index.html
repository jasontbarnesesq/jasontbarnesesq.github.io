Practice Area

EB-5 Finance

Transactional and securities counsel for EB-5 regional centers, project developers, and the foreign investors who fund them.

Overview

EB-5 financing sits where securities law, project finance, and immigration objectives meet, and its participants often span several continents.

For nearly two decades Jason has served as transactional and securities counsel in EB-5 capital raises, coordinating developers, regional centers, immigration counsel, overseas intermediaries, and escrow and fund administrators so that the investment structure supports both the project’s capital needs and each investor’s objectives.

Representative work

  • Offering structure and documents for new commercial enterprises
  • Regional center and project developer engagement terms
  • Loan and equity structures between the new commercial enterprise and the job-creating entity
  • Securities compliance for offerings to non-U.S. investors
  • Escrow, administration, and investor reporting frameworks
  • Coordination with immigration counsel and overseas intermediaries

Who we advise

Regional centers, project developers and sponsors, and investors and their advisors.

The firm provides transactional and securities counsel in EB-5 matters. Investors’ immigration petitions are prepared by their immigration counsel, with whom the firm coordinates closely.
