Approach

How engagements work.

Sophisticated work should be easy to start, easy to understand, and easy to budget. Here is what working with the firm looks like.

The process

From first call to closing.

Initial conversation

A confidential call to understand the transaction, the parties, and the timeline. We run a conflicts check before you share details.

Scope and fee proposal

A written engagement letter describing the work, the deliverables, and the fee arrangement: fixed, phased, or hourly, depending on the matter.

Execution

Jason leads the work directly and coordinates with your accountants, administrators, co-counsel, and the other side’s advisors.

Closing and handoff

Organized closing sets, clear post-closing obligations, and calendared deadlines so nothing is missed after signing.

Fees

Predictable where possible. Transparent always.

Many fund formations, offerings, and entity structuring projects have a well-defined scope, and for those the firm prefers fixed or phased fees. Where scope depends on negotiation or on the other side, work is billed hourly against an agreed estimate, with regular updates.

Every arrangement is set out in writing before work begins.

Working with your advisors

One coordinated team.

Complex capital transactions involve tax advisors, accountants, fund administrators, lenders, immigration counsel, and local counsel in other jurisdictions. The firm works alongside the advisors you already trust, and can recommend specialists when a matter needs them.

Technology-enabled delivery

A practice built on its own systems.

The firm runs on time-tracking, deadline-management, and document-standards systems that Jason designed for the way transactional work actually happens. The result is faster turnaround, consistent documents, and accurate, detailed invoices.

Client information stays in secured, access-controlled systems. Tools demonstrated in the Lab use sample data only.
