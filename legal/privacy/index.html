Privacy policy.

What this website collects, and what it does not.

Summary

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics. Fonts and images are served from this website, so visiting a page does not send your information to font or image providers.

Information you send us

When you use the contact form or the newsletter form, your email program opens a pre-filled message to the firm, or the information is sent to a form or newsletter provider the firm uses for that purpose. The firm uses this information only to respond to you, check for conflicts, and send the newsletter if you asked for it. The firm does not sell personal information.

Server logs

Like all websites, this site’s hosting provider automatically processes technical information such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver pages and keep the service secure. The firm does not use that information to identify visitors.

Newsletter

You can unsubscribe from Securities Compliance Weekly at any time using the link in any issue or by emailing jtbarnes@jtbarnespllc.com.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

The firm may update this policy and will post the revised version here. Questions can be sent to jtbarnes@jtbarnespllc.com.
