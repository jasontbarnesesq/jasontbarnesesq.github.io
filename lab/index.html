Working principles

AI is intake, not final output.

Every tool in the Lab follows the same workflow. Machines draft and organize; a lawyer reviews, decides, and signs.

GenerateDrafts, extracts, and first passes produced quickly.
NormalizeOutput conformed to one style, numbering, and terminology standard.
ValidateDefined terms, cross-references, and facts checked by a lawyer.
PackageClean deliverables in the format the reader needs.
PreservePrecedents and decisions captured for the next matter.

Tools in the Lab are shared for demonstration and general use. They are not legal services, and using them does not create an attorney-client relationship. Demonstrations use sample data only.
