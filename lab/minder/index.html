Legal Minder

Calendar management, task tracking, and deadline awareness, built for the way a legal practice actually runs.

Most calendar tools treat every appointment the same way. A client call, a filing deadline, a partner check-in, and a closing each carry different urgency, context, and follow-through. Legal Minder understands the difference.

Describe what needs to happen and when, in plain language. Minder parses it, structures it, and pushes it into the calendar you already use (iOS, Outlook, or both) without adding another app to manage.

Built as a companion to the Legal Timekeeper, Minder closes the loop between what you are tracking and what is actually scheduled.

  • Natural-language input
  • Calendar export in standard ICS format for iOS and Outlook
  • Matter and practice-area tagging
  • Reminders for post-closing and filing obligations
