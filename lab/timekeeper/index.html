Legal Timekeeper

Simple, accurate time tracking for legal work. No bloat and no learning curve, just a design built for the way a transactional practice actually operates.

Most time-tracking software assumes you work on one thing at a time in neat blocks. Legal work does not fit that model: calls interrupt drafting, emails arrive mid-review, and deadlines move. The Timekeeper was built for that reality.

  • Fast time entry with matter tagging
  • Daily, weekly, and monthly reporting
  • Plain-language narratives ready for billing review
  • Export for billing systems
  • No subscription required
